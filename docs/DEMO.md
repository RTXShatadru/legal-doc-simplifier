# Panel demonstration — 5 minutes

1. **Problem (30 seconds):** show the original rental agreement wording. Explain the goal: understandable terms and better questions to ask.
2. **Upload (45 seconds):** create an account and choose "Try a sample rental agreement", or upload your own PDF (including English/Hindi scans with OCR installed). Identify the mode badge. State explicitly whether this is the local rules demo or live Gemini analysis.
3. **Before/after (60 seconds):** open Overview, read a key term, and click its clause citation to compare it with the original extracted wording.
4. **Risk flags (60 seconds):** open Risk flags, explain the severity labels as attention levels, and follow a quote back to the source. Read the suggested clarification question.
5. **Chat (60 seconds):** ask "What does the agreement say about the security deposit?" Open the cited source. Ask about something absent from the agreement to show uncertainty.
6. **Full stack (30 seconds):** refresh the page to demonstrate persistence. Sign out, use another account, and show that its library is separate.
7. **Close (15 seconds):** download the review and explain the limitations: no legal guarantee, OCR uncertainty, and no fine-tuning in this RAG version.

Use fictional/public sample documents for a public demonstration. The supplied sample is an illustration, not a contract template to sign.

Before a live-AI demo: configure the two API keys and PostgreSQL, verify an upload/chat round trip, check provider credit/rate limits, and keep a local-demo fallback ready. Do not imply the local demo output was generated by an LLM.
